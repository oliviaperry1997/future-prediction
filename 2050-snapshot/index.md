# 2050 Snapshot: The World at Mid-Century

This document is the entry point for the 2050 milestone — a steady-state snapshot of the world at mid-century, organized by STEEP domains. It represents the endpoint of the trajectories described in the 2026-2050 transition document: the world after US dissolution, the emergence of successor states, climate transformation, and technological disruption. Each domain document describes what the world *looks like* in 2050, not how it got there.
