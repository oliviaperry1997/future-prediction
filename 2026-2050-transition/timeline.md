# 2026-2049 Timeline of Inflection Events

**Type:** `= this.type`

## Overview

The 2026-2049 period is the most transformative quarter-century since 1945-1970, and its first year is its most decisive. A dual economic shock — the Iran War (mid-2026) severing petrodollar recycling and the AI bubble burst (Q3-Q4 2026) destroying the last growth sector — shatters the fiscal foundation of the US state and triggers an acceleration of fragmentation that the later political crises only confirm. The arc moves from late-imperial US crisis through rapid collapse and fragmentation into a multi-polar post-American world where up to 19 successor-state-level entities (6 revolutionary states, 5 indigenous sovereign nations, 6 reactionary states, 2 degrading rumps) exercise varying degrees of sovereignty. A BRICS+-led alternative financial system replaces the dollar's 77-year dominance. Climate feedbacks accelerate, global population peaks, and the first binding AI governance frameworks take hold. The events below are the inflection points — moments when the trajectory shifted decisively. Each is referenced throughout the domain sections as (T-01), (T-02), and so on. See [successor-states.md](successor-states.md) for the complete reference map. West Asia inflection events (T-01c, T-01d, T-10b) trace the region's parallel arc — Iran's nuclear threshold crossing, the Quartet's post-American formalization, and Israel's terminal military-economic trajectory.

The timeline ends December 2049. The 2050 steady-state is described in Phase 3.

## Confidence Scale Reference

This document uses the following 3-tier confidence scale established in Phase 1:

- **(HIGH)** = Strong evidence across multiple domains, consistent with established trends
- **(MEDIUM)** = Plausible with supporting logic, some uncertainty
- **(LOW)** = Speculative but worth tracking; single line of evidence or contrarian signal

Confidence labels appear both in the table column and as inline badges within event descriptions.
