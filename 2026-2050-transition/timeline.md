# 2026-2049 Timeline of Inflection Events

**Type:** `= this.type`
**Status:** `= this.status`

## Overview

The 2026-2049 period is the most transformative quarter-century since 1945-1970. The arc moves from late-imperial US crisis through fragmentation into a multi-polar post-American world where at least four successor states exercise de facto sovereignty and a BRICS+-led alternative financial system challenges the dollar's 77-year dominance. Climate feedbacks accelerate, global population peaks, and the first binding AI governance frameworks take hold. The events below are the inflection points — moments when the trajectory shifted decisively. Each is referenced throughout the domain sections as (T-01), (T-02), and so on.

The timeline ends December 2049. The 2050 steady-state is described in Phase 3.

## Confidence Scale Reference

This document uses the following 3-tier confidence scale established in Phase 1:

- **(HIGH)** = Strong evidence across multiple domains, consistent with established trends
- **(MEDIUM)** = Plausible with supporting logic, some uncertainty
- **(LOW)** = Speculative but worth tracking; single line of evidence or contrarian signal

Confidence labels appear both in the table column and as inline badges within event descriptions.
