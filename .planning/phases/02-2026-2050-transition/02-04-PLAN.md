    ## Domain Pair 2: Technology → Economy (High coupling)

    Write 2-3 paragraphs:

    **Mechanism:** AI automation and energy system transformation restructure the economic base. AI displaces labor at scale, making post-capitalist economic models (UBI, public ownership of automated industries) more viable. Energy transition costs and opportunities shape which successor states thrive economically.

    **Specific loops:**
    - AI automation (technology Driver 1) drives labor displacement, making socialist policies (UBI, nationalized automated industries) more electorally viable in progressive successor states — directly enabling economy Driver 3 (socialist transitions) (linked to T-03, T-12)
    - Energy system transformation (technology Driver 2) determines which successor states have viable post-carbon economies — Gulf Compact's oil dependence becomes a liability, Pacific Republic's renewable endowment becomes an asset (linked to T-09, T-12)
    - BRICS+ alternative financial system (T-10) is technology-enabled (digital currencies, blockchain settlement), creating a feedback loop where tech infrastructure enables economic de-dollarization, which further reduces US successor states' financial constraints

    **Confidence:** HIGH — structural coupling is strong and direction clear

    ## Domain Pair 3: Demographics → Culture (Medium coupling)

    Write 2-3 paragraphs:

    **Mechanism:** Demographic change (migration, aging, urbanization) alters the cultural landscape. Climate migrants bring different values and beliefs to host regions. Aging populations become more culturally conservative. Urban/rural demographic divergence drives cultural divergence between successor states.

    **Specific loops:**
    - Large-scale climate migration (demographics Driver 2, T-07) into progressive regions reinforces their progressive culture while migration into conservative regions triggers nativist backlash — both outcomes intensify the cultural divergence described in culture Driver 1 (national identity erosion) (linked to T-07)
    - Population aging (demographics Driver 1, T-11) shifts cultural priorities toward stability, healthcare, and intergenerational equity — these become defining values in older successor states (Northeast Corridor) while younger successor states (Gulf Compact) develop different cultural profiles
    - Urbanization and coastal abandonment (demographics Driver 4) concentrates progressive populations in interior "climate haven" cities while conservative populations remain in climate-vulnerable coastal/rural areas — reinforcing the geographic sorting that underlies culture Driver 3 (post-capitalist ideology)

    **Confidence:** MEDIUM — coupling operates on longer timescales with significant feedback delay
