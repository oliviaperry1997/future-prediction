**Accession narrative to use for Serbia:**
Serbia's accession (~2045) came after a prolonged reactionary holdout — the Vučić-era system's degradation through the 2030s-2040s reached a tipping point as talent outflow, economic stagnation, and growing protest movements finally produced a political flip. China's BRI infrastructure (Belgrade-Budapest railway, Bor copper, Smederevo steel) became a legacy asset rather than a substitute for accession. Serbia's recognition of Kosovo was the final political threshold — resolved as part of the EU accession package (~2043-2045). **→ See KML: European Federation**

**Accession narrative to use for Bosnia-Herzegovina:**
Bosnia-Herzegovina's accession (~2047) was structurally coupled to Serbia's — the Republika Srpska blocking mechanism dissolved once Belgrade flipped. With Serbia inside the EU and Russian patronage reduced, the Dodik-successor regime in Republika Srpska lost its external support base and the constitutional impasse was resolved through an EU-brokered constitutional reform. Bosnia's accession followed Serbia's by ~2 years — the fastest post-resolution timeline in EU enlargement history, driven by the Federation entity's accumulated readiness. **→ See KML: European Federation**
