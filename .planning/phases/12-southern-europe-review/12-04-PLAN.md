**Southern Europe climate — key facts (from 12-CONTEXT.md canonical refs):**
- Mediterranean heat stress: Southern Europe is one of the most climate-vulnerable regions globally
- Desertification: Spain (especially southeast/Murcia/Almería), southern Italy (Sicily, Calabria), Greece (Crete, Peloponnese)
- Wildfire risk: High across all Mediterranean coastal areas; extreme fire seasons by 2030s-2050s
- Sea-level rise (0.3-0.5m by 2050): Malta (low-lying island) and Cyprus coastlines most affected
- Water scarcity: Southern Spain, Greece (Aegean islands, Attica), and coastal areas
- Mediterranean Sea temperature rise — marine ecosystem stress, increased hurricane-like medicanes

**Mediterranean regional framing (if not already present):**
Add or update a Mediterranean climate entry establishing: the Mediterranean basin is experiencing 1.5-2x the global average warming rate; summer temperatures regularly exceed 45-48°C in southern Spain, southern Italy, and Greece by the 2040s-2050s; the Mediterranean Sea surface temperature has risen ~2-3°C from pre-industrial baseline; extreme precipitation events (atmospheric rivers, medicanes — Mediterranean tropical-like cyclones) have increased in frequency.

**Spain:** Desertification of the southeast (Murcia, Almería, eastern Andalusia) — advancing desertification front threatening agricultural production in one of Europe's key food export regions; water scarcity (Segura and Jucar river basins); Canary Islands as climate-affected Atlantic archipelago (different climate envelope than mainland); wildfire regime (Galicia and Castile particularly affected).

**Greece:** Mediterranean heat stress at its most severe in mainland Greece — Athens regularly exceeds 42°C in summer by 2040s; Crete and Aegean islands (including Turkish-occupied Eastern Aegean islands — note the climate situation regardless of political control) experiencing semi-arid conditions; wildfire regime (Attica, Peloponnese, Rhodes); water scarcity across the mainland and islands; the frozen Aegean conflict means the Eastern Aegean islands are Turkish-occupied but still geographically Greek Mediterranean — climate crisis affects the contested territory.

**Malta:** Sea-level rise critical for a low-lying island (~200m max elevation, most infrastructure at 5-30m); 0.3-0.5m rise by 2050 threatens coastal infrastructure; summer heat extremes (island has limited cooling from the sea by mid-century); water scarcity (Malta already imports water via desalination and reverse osmosis — desalination capacity expansion critical).

**Cyprus:** Sea-level rise on eastern Mediterranean coastline; summer heat extremes (Nicosia regularly 40°C+); water scarcity intensifies in the south (Republic of Cyprus); climate note: the EEZ gas fields being exploited under the revenue-sharing arrangement are in climate-stressed waters experiencing ocean acidification and temperature rise.

**Portugal:** Atlantic position moderates the worst Mediterranean heat; but the Alentejo and Algarve regions experience desertification pressure; wildfire regime (2017-scale megafires become the norm); droughts affecting cork oak and olive production.

**Croatia and Slovenia:** Adriatic coastal heat stress (Dalmatian coast tourist season disrupted by extreme heat); Alpine glacier retreat (Slovenia); sea-level rise on Croatian Adriatic coast.

**Western Balkans — sovereign entities:** Shared Balkan climate context — extreme heat events in summer, flash flood risk from atmospheric rivers, agricultural stress. Specific notes for coastal Montenegro, and inland Serbia/North Macedonia.
