<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### KML Geometry

- **D-A:** Replace point-in-polygon assignment with **longitudinal sector clips**. The current approach assigns each existing GADM polygon patch to a claim zone based on longitude of its centroid. The correct approach: clip polygons by longitudinal sectors (e.g., 45°E–136°E for AAT). A polygon that spans sectors gets split at the sector boundary line, producing distinct polygon fragments per zone. This produces correct sector-boundary rendering in Google Earth.
- **D-B:** Clip the **Argentine-Chilean Peninsula** polygon to its actual sector (53°W–90°W). Currently it covers the entire Antarctic mainland because the GADM patches in that zone were not sector-filtered. The sector clip must trim polygons at 53°W and 90°W meridians.
- **D-C:** Ice-shelf-based geometry (SCAR ADD dataset) remains a **future refinement**. The current GADM coastline geometry is used with longitudinal sector clips as an intermediate step. SCAR ADD data is not programmatically downloadable.

### Entity Restructuring

- **D-D:** Claim zones are **nested under their owning entities** in both KML folder hierarchy and entity-config.json. The current structure has 7 Antarctica claim zones as top-level entities. The new structure makes them children of the states that administer them:
  - Australian Antarctic Territory → nested under Australia
  - Ross Dependency → nested under New Zealand
  - Adélie Land → nested under European Federation (via France)
  - Dronning Maud Land → nested under European Federation (via Norway)
  - Chinese Marie Byrd Land Zone → nested under China
  - Argentine-Chilean Joint Peninsula → **stays standalone** (it's a joint claim, not a unitary state)
  - British Antarctic Territory → **removed** (claim is defunct, not dormant)
- **D-E:** Claim zone colors remain distinct from parent entity colors. Each zone keeps its own KML style (the 6 styles from Phase 19). The parent entities (Australia, NZ, EF, China) continue using their existing colors for non-Antarctic territory. The KML folder hierarchy shows the nesting structure visually.
- **D-F:** entity-config.json `folder_hierarchy` reflects nesting: Australia's hierarchy gets a new child `"Australian Antarctic Territory"`, NZ gets `"Ross Dependency"`, European Federation gets `"Adélie Land"` and `"Dronning Maud Land"`, China gets `"Chinese Marie Byrd Land Zone"`. Argentine-Chilean Joint Peninsula remains a top-level entry. British Antarctic Territory entity is removed entirely.

### Narrative Corrections

- **D-G:** UK claim is **defunct** (not dormant). It died when the UK failed to defend the Falklands, and formally ended when England went revolutionary. The British Antarctic Survey (BAS) may continue science operations but it has no territorial claim status. All references to "dormant paper claim" → "defunct — claim dissolved with UK sovereignty" across all 6 domain docs. British Antarctic Territory removed from entity-config, user_colors, borders.kml. Its former sector (20°W–53°W) is reallocated: the Peninsula portion already absorbed by Argentine-Chilean joint administration; the Atlantic-facing sector becomes unadministered Antarctic territory.
- **D-H:** Australia **auctions/leases portions of AAT** it cannot police. Australia completed its $371M infrastructure renewal but cannot project authority across 42% of the continent. Some territory within the AAT is leased or auctioned to other states — likely including India (who already has stations in the sector) and possibly other BRICS+ states. The narrative is updated in borders-geopolitics.md and economy.md to reflect this. The AAT in borders.kml remains one claim zone but the narrative describes the leasing arrangement.

### Format & Consistency

- **D-I:** All domain doc entries updated for defunct UK, Australia auctioning, entity nesting. Phase 19 entries in borders-geopolitics.md, economy.md, demographics.md, culture.md, climate.md, technology.md are revised to:
  - Remove British Antarctic Territory references (replace with unadministered sector)
  - Update UK status from dormant → defunct
  - Add Australia AAT leasing/auction narrative
  - Ensure claim zone names match the new nested entity structure
- **D-J:** `user_colors.json` removes the British Antarctic Territory entry. The remaining 6 claim zone entries are updated to reflect their parent entity relationships (the color entries themselves don't change values, only their position/context — or stay as-is with notes that they're claim-zone sub-entities).

### Plan Structure

- **D-K:** Two waves. Wave 1: KML geometry (sector clips, Argentina-Chilean clip, entity nesting in KML/entity-config/user_colors, BAT removal). Wave 2: Narrative updates across all 6 domain docs.

</decisions>
