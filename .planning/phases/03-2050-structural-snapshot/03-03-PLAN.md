    ### Key Changes From Previous Milestone

    3-5 bullets summarizing technological transformation between the early-2020s baseline and 2050:
    - AI has become fully integrated into governance, economic planning, and daily life — with divergent regulatory regimes across successor states
    - Energy transition completed in most revolutionary states; reactionary states lag with hybrid fossil/renewable systems
    - Biotechnology reached maturity: gene editing, synthetic biology, and longevity research have produced measurable demographic effects

    Organize by technology domain:

    #### AI & Computation

    - AI governance regimes: divergent approaches across US successor states (→ See prediction-010), EU-style regulation, BRICS+ framework

    #### Energy Systems
    - Global energy mix as of 2050: renewable penetration, nuclear status, fossil fuel residual
    - Revolutionary states (PPR, NEC, GLPR): near-100% renewable with advanced grid/storage
    - Reactionary states (Gulf Compact, Atlantic South): hybrid systems, continued fossil extraction
    - Nuclear fusion: experimental vs. commercial status
    - Grid architecture: decentralized/distributed vs. centralized, microgrid proliferation
    - Energy storage at scale: battery, hydrogen, pumped hydro, novel storage

    #### Biotechnology & Health
    - Gene editing (CRISPR and successors): therapeutic applications, agricultural applications, governance
    - Synthetic biology: industrial biomanufacturing status
    - Longevity research: average lifespan increases, anti-aging therapies
    - Pandemic preparedness and bio-surveillance systems
    - Health system divergence: revolutionary states with universal healthcare vs. reactionary states with market-based systems

    #### Information & Communication
    - Internet fragmentation: successor state information boundaries, firewalls, data localization
    - Social media and information ecosystem evolution post-2030s fragmentation
    - Surveillance and privacy regimes across different state types
    - Communication infrastructure: satellite internet (Starlink-class), fiber, mesh networks

    #### Space & Orbital Infrastructure
    - Commercial space stations and habitats
    - Lunar presence: bases, resource extraction, governance regime
    - Satellite infrastructure: earth observation, communications, navigation
    - Space debris and orbital governance
    - Moon and orbital territory claims

    #### Transportation
    - Electric/autonomous vehicle penetration
    - Aviation: sustainable fuel status, electric aviation
    - High-speed rail networks
    - Maritime shipping: energy transition, autonomous vessels

    #### Advanced Manufacturing
    - 3D printing/additive manufacturing at scale
    - Robotics and automation in industry
    - Supply chain reconfiguration post-fragmentation
