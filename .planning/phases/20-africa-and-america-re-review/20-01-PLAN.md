**Objective:** Determine which of Nigeria's 36 states + FCT go to AES vs Nigeria rump per D-04 criteria: Muslim-majority → AES, rest → Nigeria, both zones must be contiguous, FCT stays with Nigeria.

**Step 1 — Research Nigeria state religious demographics:**
Use web research to find the most reliable available data on Nigeria's state-by-state religious demographics. Key sources:
- Pew Research Center (most comprehensive — "Tolerance and Tension: Islam and Christianity in Sub-Saharan Africa", 2010; "The Future of World Religions", 2015)
- Afrobarometer survey data (Round 7-9, 2017-2023, religious affiliation by state)
- Nigeria National Population Commission census data (2006 census with religious questions, 2016 census estimates)
- CHART (Christian-Muslim ratio in Nigeria) data
- Wikipedia "Religion in Nigeria by state" or similar summary data

Sources must be cited with publication year. Do not rely on unsourced claims.

**Step 2 — Apply D-04 criteria:**
For each of Nigeria's 36 states + FCT:
- If the state is Muslim-majority (>50% Muslim) → assign to AES candidate set
- If the state is Christian-majority or mixed (Muslim plurality but <50%) → assign to Nigeria candidate set
- FCT (Abuja) → forced to Nigeria per D-04 regardless of demographics

**Step 3 — Apply contiguity constraint:**
From the Muslim-majority candidate set:
- Verify that resulting AES territory forms a single contiguous block (no enclaves/exclaves)
- If contiguity fails, identify which Muslim-majority states must be retained or released to maintain contiguity
- Document the contiguity reasoning explicitly

From the Nigeria candidate set:
- Verify remaining states form a single contiguous block
- FCT must be geographically reachable from the Nigeria block
