# Phase 13 Plan 03: Levant Cluster Profiles Summary

## What Was Done

### Task 1: Israel expansion + Levant Republic profile

**Israel** entry expanded from a single-sentence stub to a ~250-word full profile covering:
- Nuclear-armed coastal rump geography (Haifa + Central + Tel Aviv districts, Ashkelon terminus)
- Dimona/Negev nuclear infrastructure as the existential core under the cascade mechanism
- Three mechanisms of contraction: (1) emigration cascade — skilled/younger Israelis departing; (2) interceptor gap — cheap drone swarms overwhelm expensive interceptor economics, US resupply guarantee dissolved; (3) manpower crisis — reserve call-up unsustainable, West Bank settler military overhead net liability

- Regional nuclear dynamics: Israeli deterrent alongside Saudi threshold (~2040s, Pakistani assistance) and Iranian capability (tested mid-2030s)
- Stage 3-4 Reactionary designation with cross-references

**Levant Republic** new entry (~280 words) written covering:

- Jordan mechanism: Hashemite three-leg structural collapse (US guarantee, Gulf patronage, Hashemite formula obsolete once Palestine sovereign)
- Lebanon mechanism: Hezbollah repositioning from Iran proxy to national movement; Lebanon corridor as territorial mechanism
- Timeline: ~2038-2045 merger window, ~60-70% probability complete by 2050
- AUF nucleus designation
- KML treatment: merged polygon, individual placemarks deprecated
- Redirect notes added for Palestine, Jordan, Lebanon as standalone entries

### Task 2: Syria, Iraq, AUF entries

**Syria** profile (~220 words) written covering:
- Managed tri-partite equilibrium: Turkish north, Iranian east/coast, nominal Damascus
- US collapse impact: Turkey's Syria attention deficit, Iranian influence expansion

- Latent AUF member, formal integration post-2055

**Iraq** profile (~200 words) written covering:

- Iran-axis deepest integration; AUF-PMF coordination
- AUF associated membership complicated by Kurdish question (KRG ideological distance)
- KRG as distinct sub-entity: Ankara oil pipeline dependency, not AUF-affiliated

**Arab United Front (AUF)** meta-entry (~400 words) written covering:
- ALM/ARCC organizational form ~2031-2033
- Three-tier structure: Strategic Council, Country Coordination Committees, Functional Networks
- Founding Congress ~2031-2033 in Palestinian city; Arab Liberation Charter adopted
- Founding actors: Palestine, Yemen/Ansarallah, Lebanon/Hezbollah
- Feedback loop acceleration: Jordan (3-7yr), Egypt (~2043-2048), Libya (Egypt+3-5yr), Saudi fragmentation/Hejaz, Syria (long game)
- At 2050 snapshot: AUF + Levant Republic operational; Yemen + Libya full members; Egypt accession; Iraq associated; Syria candidate; Hejaz pre-accession
- Iran relationship: cooperative strategic alignment
- External intervention risk: US-Israeli-Gulf coalition 2035-2042 window
